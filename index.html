About Me

Er. Madhab Rijal is a Professional Engineer, Assistant Professor at Mid-West University (Nepal), and a PhD candidate in Hydraulic Engineering at Chang'an University, China.

His research focuses on hydrology, environmental science, and climate extremes. He has authored 8+ peer-reviewed papers in SCI-indexed journals including Archives of Computational Methods in Engineering, Science of the Total Environment, and Journal of Hydrology.

Proficient in ArcGIS, Origin Pro, SWAT, J2000, Python, R, and remote sensing, he combines field expertise with advanced computation to address water security challenges.
